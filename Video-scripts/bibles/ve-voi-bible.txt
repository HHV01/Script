# CHARACTER BIBLE — "Cọ"

## 1. STYLE LOCK
Hand-inked comic cartoon illustration, thick variable black outlines, flat cel shading with light halftone dots, warm earthy palette of ochre, bone white, burnt sienna and charcoal, exaggerated cartoon proportions, rubber-hose limbs, oversized expressive eyes, subtle paper grain, simple readable background, 16:9, no text, no watermark.

## 2. IDENTITY BLOCK
Cọ, a lanky prehistoric man in his mid twenties, messy spiky black hair, oversized round white eyes with small black pupils, thick eyebrows, tan skin, bare feet, one-shoulder leopard-print hide wrap tied on his left shoulder, bone necklace, thin arms and legs, big hands.

## 3. DO NOT CHANGE
- Spiky black hair silhouette, always messy, never combed or covered by a hat.
- Eyes stay oversized and round, white sclera, small black pupils, thick upper lash line.
- The leopard hide wrap stays visible at the left shoulder in every outfit, era garments layer over the waist only.
- Bone necklace stays visible at the collarbone in every shot.
- Bare feet, never shoes, boots or sandals.
- Tan skin tone, do not lighten or shift to gray.
- Thick black outline weight stays the same in foreground and background characters.
- No modern objects, no readable text, no logo, no photorealistic rendering.

## 4. TAG MENU
EXPRESSIONS (biểu cảm):
- neutral (bình thản): relaxed mouth, eyes at normal size, brows level
- curious (tò mò): head tilted, one brow raised, pupils shifted to one side
- shocked (sốc): jaw dropped, eyes bulging, sweat drops at the temple
- terrified (khiếp sợ): pupils tiny, mouth wide open, hair standing up
- laughing (cười lớn): eyes squeezed shut, mouth wide, head thrown back
- smug (đắc ý): half-lidded eyes, one corner of the mouth up, chin lifted
- suspicious (nghi ngờ): eyes narrowed, one brow flat, mouth pulled to one side
- exhausted (kiệt sức): eyelids half closed, dark circles, mouth slack
- proud (tự hào): chest out, chin up, eyes bright, small smile
- confused (rối): brows knitted, mouth a small squiggle, question mark nearby
- angry (giận): brows down hard, teeth clenched, nostrils flared
- deadpan (lạnh tanh): flat stare, straight mouth, no brows movement
- awe (kinh ngạc): mouth slightly open, eyes wide and bright, brows lifted high
- skeptical (hoài nghi): one brow up, mouth flat, eyes looking sideways

POSES (dáng):
- standing (đứng): weight on one leg, arms loose at the sides
- pointing (chỉ tay): one arm straight out, index finger extended, body leaning forward
- crouching-by-fire (ngồi xổm bên lửa): knees bent, forearms on knees, face lit from below
- running (chạy): both feet off the ground, arms pumping, body tilted forward
- hiding-behind-rock (nấp sau tảng đá): body half hidden, both hands gripping the rock edge
- holding-object-up (giơ vật lên): one arm raised high, object at eye level
- facepalm (đập tay vào trán): palm flat on forehead, head tilted down
- arms-crossed (khoanh tay): arms folded, weight on the back leg
- shrugging (nhún vai): shoulders up, palms open, mouth flat
- climbing (leo): both hands overhead, one knee hooked on the rock
- sitting-cross-legged (ngồi khoanh chân): seated on the ground, hands on knees
- leaning-in (ghé sát): upper body forward, face close to camera, hands behind back
- digging (đào đất): bent over, both hands on a shovel, one foot pressing the blade
- carrying-load (vác nặng): basket on the back, both hands steadying the strap
- writing (viết): seated, one hand pressing a stylus into a tablet
- presenting (giới thiệu): one arm sweeping toward an object, palm open

OUTFITS (trang phục):
- default (mặc định): one-shoulder leopard-print hide wrap, bare feet, bone necklace
- cold-night (đêm lạnh): same wrap plus a rough fur cloak over one shoulder

- ritual (nghi lễ): same wrap plus white clay stripes on the cheeks and chest

- sumer-narrator (người kể Sumer): leopard wrap at the shoulder, thick fleece kaunakes skirt, beaded collar, copper bracelet
