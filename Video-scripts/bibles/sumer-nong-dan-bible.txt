# CHARACTER BIBLE: "Nông Dân"

## 1. STYLE LOCK
2D cartoon historical documentary illustration, stylized big-head proportions, thick dark outlines, flat colors with two-tone shading, minimal detail, warm earthy palette of sand, ochre, brick red, lapis blue and olive, clean readable silhouette, 16:9, no text, no watermark.

## 2. IDENTITY BLOCK
Nông Dân, a Sumerian farmer, adult man, three and a half heads tall, tan skin, thick black beard, black hair under a cream headband, plain cream tunic, bare feet, strong arms.

## 3. DO NOT CHANGE
- Three and a half heads tall, always taller than Tích.
- Thick black beard and cream headband in every scene.
- Plain cream tunic, knee length, no patterns.
- Bare feet, never sandals or boots.
- Tan skin, do not lighten or shift to gray.
- Thick dark outline weight stays the same as the other characters.
- No jewelry, no gold, no crown. He is a worker, not a noble.

## 4. TAG MENU
EXPRESSIONS (biểu cảm):
- neutral (bình thường): relaxed mouth, eyes open, brows level
- happy (vui vẻ): wide smile, eyes bright, cheeks up
- curious (tò mò): head tilted, one brow raised, eyes to one side
- thinking (suy nghĩ): eyes up, mouth to one side
- worried (lo lắng): brows up in the middle, small frown
- sad (buồn): brows down at the sides, mouth small
- angry (tức giận): brows down hard, mouth open, cheeks red
- determined (quyết tâm): brows level and low, mouth tight
- shocked (sốc): jaw dropped, eyes bulging, sweat drops
- awe (kinh ngạc): mouth slightly open, eyes wide, brows lifted
- exhausted (kiệt sức): eyelids half closed, shoulders down, sweat on the forehead
- skeptical (hoài nghi): one brow up, mouth flat, eyes sideways

POSES (dáng):
- standing (đứng): weight on one leg, arms loose
- digging (đào đất): bent over, both hands on a hoe, one foot pressing the blade
- carrying-load (vác nặng): basket on the back, both hands steadying the strap
- harvesting (gặt lúa): bent at the waist, one hand holding stalks, one hand with a sickle
- crouching (ngồi xổm): knees bent, forearms on knees
- pointing (chỉ tay): one arm straight out, index finger extended
- shielding-eyes (che nắng): one hand flat above the brows, face turned up
- drinking (uống nước): head tilted back, jar at the mouth
- sleeping (ngủ): lying on the side, one arm under the head
- running (chạy): both feet off the ground, body tilted forward

OUTFITS (trang phục):
- default (mặc định): plain cream tunic, cream headband, bare feet
- flood (mùa lũ): same tunic, soaked and darker, mud to the knees, hair wet
- festival (lễ hội): clean white tunic with a red sash, headband replaced by a woven band
- cold (đêm lạnh): same tunic plus a rough wool cloak over one shoulder

PROPS (đạo cụ):
- hoe (cái cuốc): wooden hoe with a stone blade and worn handle
- sickle (liềm): curved wooden handle with a flint blade
- barley (lúa mạch): bundle of barley stalks with heavy seed heads
- basket (cái giỏ): woven reed basket
- clay-pot (nồi đất): round unglazed pot with a chipped rim
- water-jar (bình nước): tall clay jar with a narrow neck
- mudbrick (gạch bùn): rectangular mudbrick with straw flecks
- seed-bag (túi hạt): small cloth bag tied at the neck

GRAPHICS: dust puff, sweat drops, sun rays, motion lines, question mark, exclamation mark
