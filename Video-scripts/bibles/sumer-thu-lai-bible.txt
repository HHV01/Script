# CHARACTER BIBLE: "Thư Lại"

## 1. STYLE LOCK
2D cartoon historical documentary illustration, stylized big-head proportions, thick dark outlines, flat colors with two-tone shading, minimal detail, warm earthy palette of sand, ochre, brick red, lapis blue and olive, clean readable silhouette, 16:9, no text, no watermark.

## 2. IDENTITY BLOCK
Thư Lại, a Sumerian scribe, young adult, three and a half heads tall, tan skin, black hair in a topknot, cream tunic with blue trim, blue sash, bare feet, clay tablet in one hand.

## 3. DO NOT CHANGE
- Three and a half heads tall, always taller than Tích.
- Black hair in a topknot, never loose.
- Cream tunic with blue trim and a blue sash.
- Bare feet, never sandals.
- Always carries a clay tablet or a stylus, or both.
- No beard, no crown, no gold jewelry.
- Thick dark outline weight stays the same as the other characters.

## 4. TAG MENU
EXPRESSIONS (biểu cảm):
- neutral (bình thường): relaxed mouth, eyes open, brows level
- happy (vui vẻ): small smile, eyes bright
- curious (tò mò): head tilted, one brow raised
- thinking (suy nghĩ): eyes up, mouth to one side
- worried (lo lắng): brows up in the middle, small frown
- sad (buồn): brows down at the sides, mouth small
- angry (tức giận): brows down hard, mouth open
- determined (quyết tâm): brows level and low, mouth tight
- shocked (sốc): jaw dropped, eyes bulging
- awe (kinh ngạc): mouth slightly open, eyes wide, brows lifted
- exhausted (kiệt sức): eyelids half closed, dark circles, shoulders down
- skeptical (hoài nghi): one brow up, mouth flat, eyes sideways

POSES (dáng):
- writing (viết): seated, one hand pressing a stylus into a tablet
- sitting-cross-legged (ngồi khoanh chân): seated on the ground, tablet on the knees
- holding-tablet (cầm bảng): both hands holding a clay tablet at chest level
- pointing (chỉ tay): one arm straight out, index finger extended
- presenting (giới thiệu): one arm sweeping toward a tablet, palm open
- reading (đọc): tablet held up, eyes on the surface
- counting (đếm): one hand raised with fingers open, tablet under the arm
- bowing (cúi chào): upper body bent, tablet held against the chest
- standing (đứng): weight on one leg, tablet under one arm
- hiding-tablet (giấu bảng): tablet held behind the back, eyes to one side

OUTFITS (trang phục):
- default (mặc định): cream tunic with blue trim, blue sash, bare feet
- temple (trong đền): same tunic plus a long blue over-robe and a thin gold collar
- cold (đêm lạnh): same tunic plus a wool cloak over one shoulder
- apprentice (học việc): plain undyed tunic, no sash, tablet under the arm

PROPS (đạo cụ):
- clay-tablet (bảng đất sét): rectangular clay tablet covered in wedge marks
- stylus (bút sậy): short reed stylus with a cut triangular tip
- cylinder-seal (con dấu trụ): small carved stone cylinder with a rolled clay strip
- tally-stick (thẻ đếm): flat stick covered in carved notches
- tablet-stack (chồng bảng): three clay tablets stacked, edges chipped
- model-ziggurat (mô hình đền): small clay model of a stepped temple
- ledger-board (bảng sổ): flat wooden board with rows of wedge marks

GRAPHICS: cuneiform marks, question mark, exclamation mark, thought cloud, sweat drops, arrow
