# CHARACTER BIBLE: "Tích"

## 1. STYLE LOCK
2D cartoon historical documentary illustration, stylized big-head proportions, thick dark outlines, flat colors with two-tone shading, minimal detail, warm earthy palette of sand, ochre, brick red, lapis blue and olive, clean readable silhouette, 16:9, no text, no watermark.

## 2. IDENTITY BLOCK
Tích, a Vietnamese boy around ten, round face, big dark eyes, short messy black hair, yellow hoodie, dark blue jeans, white sneakers, small brown backpack, three heads tall.

## 3. DO NOT CHANGE
- Three heads tall, always shorter than the adults, head reaches their chest.
- Round face, big dark eyes, short messy black hair.
- Yellow hoodie and small brown backpack in every scene unless the outfit says otherwise.
- White sneakers, never sandals or boots.
- Thick dark outline weight stays the same as the adults.
- No realistic shading, no gradient, no 3D render.
- No text on the hoodie, no logo, no brand mark.

## 4. TAG MENU
EXPRESSIONS (biểu cảm):
- neutral (bình thường): relaxed mouth, eyes open, brows level
- happy (vui vẻ): wide smile, eyes bright, cheeks up
- curious (tò mò): head tilted, one brow raised, eyes to one side
- thinking (suy nghĩ): eyes up, mouth to one side, hand near chin
- worried (lo lắng): brows up in the middle, small frown, hands close to chest
- sad (buồn): brows down at the sides, mouth small, eyes wet
- angry (tức giận): brows down hard, mouth open, cheeks red
- determined (quyết tâm): brows level and low, mouth tight, eyes forward
- shocked (sốc): jaw dropped, eyes bulging, sweat drops at the temple
- awe (kinh ngạc): mouth slightly open, eyes wide and bright, brows lifted high
- exhausted (kiệt sức): eyelids half closed, shoulders down, mouth slack
- skeptical (hoài nghi): one brow up, mouth flat, eyes looking sideways

POSES (dáng):
- standing (đứng): weight on one leg, arms loose
- pointing (chỉ tay): one arm straight out, index finger extended
- walking (đi bộ): one foot forward, arms swinging
- running (chạy): both feet off the ground, body tilted forward
- holding-tablet (cầm bảng): both hands holding a clay tablet at chest level
- arms-crossed (khoanh tay): arms folded, weight on the back leg
- facepalm (đập tay vào trán): palm flat on forehead, head tilted down
- shrugging (nhún vai): shoulders up, palms open, mouth flat
- sitting (ngồi): seated on the ground, hands on knees
- presenting (giới thiệu): one arm sweeping toward an object, palm open
- leaning-in (ghé sát): upper body forward, face close to camera

OUTFITS (trang phục):
- default (mặc định): yellow hoodie, dark blue jeans, white sneakers, brown backpack
- rain (mưa): yellow raincoat over the hoodie, hood up, wet jeans

PROPS (đạo cụ):

- stylus (bút sậy): short reed stylus with a cut tip

- magnifying-glass (kính lúp): small round magnifying glass with a wooden handle
