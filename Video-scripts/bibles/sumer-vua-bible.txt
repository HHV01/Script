# CHARACTER BIBLE: "Vua Sumer"

## 1. STYLE LOCK
2D cartoon historical documentary illustration, stylized big-head proportions, thick dark outlines, flat colors with two-tone shading, minimal detail, warm earthy palette of sand, ochre, brick red, lapis blue and olive, clean readable silhouette, 16:9, no text, no watermark.

## 2. IDENTITY BLOCK
Vua Sumer, a Sumerian king, adult man, three and a half heads tall, tan skin, thick black beard, tall blue and gold crown, blue robe with gold trim, gold armbands, white pleated skirt, bull-head staff.

## 3. DO NOT CHANGE
- Three and a half heads tall, always taller than Tích.
- Tall blue and gold crown, never removed.
- Thick black beard with tight curls.
- Blue robe with gold trim over a white pleated skirt.
- Gold armbands on both wrists.
- Carries the bull-head staff in formal scenes.
- Thick dark outline weight stays the same as the other characters.
- No Greek cloak, no laurel wreath, no Roman armor.

## 4. TAG MENU
EXPRESSIONS (biểu cảm):
- neutral (bình thường): relaxed mouth, eyes open, brows level
- happy (vui vẻ): small smile, eyes bright, chin up
- curious (tò mò): head tilted, one brow raised
- thinking (suy nghĩ): eyes up, mouth to one side
- worried (lo lắng): brows up in the middle, small frown
- sad (buồn): brows down at the sides, mouth small
- angry (tức giận): brows down hard, teeth clenched
- determined (quyết tâm): brows level and low, mouth tight
- shocked (sốc): jaw dropped, eyes bulging
- awe (kinh ngạc): mouth slightly open, eyes wide, brows lifted
- exhausted (kiệt sức): eyelids half closed, shoulders down
- skeptical (hoài nghi): one brow up, mouth flat, eyes sideways

POSES (dáng):
- standing (đứng): weight on one leg, staff planted
- throne (ngồi ngai): seated on a low mudbrick throne, staff across the knees
- pointing (chỉ tay): one arm straight out, index finger extended
- arms-raised (giơ hai tay): both arms up, palms open
- holding-staff (cầm quyền trượng): both hands on the staff, staff vertical
- inspecting (quan sát): leaning forward, one hand on the chin
- walking (đi bộ): one foot forward, robe swinging
- decreeing (ban lệnh): one arm raised with a clay tablet, chin up

OUTFITS (trang phục):
- default (mặc định): blue robe with gold trim, white pleated skirt, gold armbands, tall blue and gold crown
- war (ra trận): same crown, copper helmet under it, plain blue tunic, leather belt, no skirt
- ritual (nghi lễ): white robe with gold trim, wide gold collar, same crown
- mourning (để tang): plain undyed robe, no gold, same crown

PROPS (đạo cụ):
- staff (quyền trượng): tall staff with a bull-head top and a wrapped grip
- mace (chùy): stone mace head on a wooden handle
- clay-tablet (bảng đất sét): large clay tablet covered in wedge marks
- cylinder-seal (con dấu trụ): carved stone cylinder with a rolled clay strip
- model-ziggurat (mô hình đền): small clay model of a stepped temple
- tribute-basket (giỏ cống nạp): woven basket filled with grain and small jars

GRAPHICS: sun rays, exclamation mark, arrow, red annotation circle, dust puff, cuneiform marks
